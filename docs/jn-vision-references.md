# JN scenes and founder quotations

The JN archive supplies real landscape and trip references for Odle's vision. The quotations below are exact excerpts from the founder's statements in the October 16, 2026 vision conversation. They are paired editorially with archive scenes; they are not quotations transcribed from the video audio.

## Washington origin and shared effort

**Winchester Mountain, Washington.** `JN0244-02`, original file `GX014578 [16413058 +1945].mov`, selected source interval 9.300-19.469 seconds. The reviewed image shows two hikers on a rocky autumn trail beneath mountain peaks.

> friends you know you will spend a lifetime of adventures with

This is the lead landscape on `/vision`. It connects the Washington origin with companionship and terrain. The archive image establishes the visible scene; the quote describes the founder's aspiration for lasting friendship.

## Snow and moving water

**Park Butte, Washington.** `JN0189-01`, original file `GX014758 [19285220 +1007].mov`, selected source interval 8.192-10.043 seconds. Snow is visibly falling around a backpacker crossing a rocky creek.

> warm nights in cold weather

On the website this line accompanies the question of comfort during movement and when stopping. The image is a snowy creek crossing in daylight. It does not establish nighttime temperature, the wearer's comfort, or any garment's insulation or waterproofing.

## Rock and freedom of movement

**Thousand Island Lake approach, California.** `JN0075-01`, original file `GX015068.mov`, selected source interval 13.180-27.243 seconds. A hiker is stepping across angular loose rock on a mountainside.

> Unrestricted mobility and a second skin

This scene supports discussion of articulation, abrasion, and construction as development priorities. It does not show glissading or establish fabric performance. The founder's separate descriptions of sliding on glaciated mountains, wedging knees between rocks, and descending scree remain use cases to investigate.

## Ease at the destination

**Thousand Island Lake, California.** `JN0129-05`, original file `GX015115.mov`, selected source interval 34.935-38.221 seconds. A person fishes from the shore of a calm lake with peaks reflected in the water.

> truly rewarding and relaxing when you reach your destination

This is the clearest visual counterpart to the feeling at the heart of the brand. The image records a pause at the lake; the interpretation of ease comes from the founder's vision.

## Making camp together

**Thousand Island Lake trip, California.** `JN0112-08`, original file `GX015105.mov`, selected source interval 57.925-70.219 seconds. Two people prepare a tent in a meadow under a cloud-filled sky.

> push yourself with heart in hand

The scene connects effort with companionship and settling in. The website adapts the line into ordinary prose as “pushing yourself with heart in hand,” without presenting the adaptation as a verbatim quote.

## Trust as the long-term ambition

The founder's full statement of the central feeling:

> That feeling when you are in the mountains and are damn fucking glad you invested your hard earned money on the gear that makes your excursion truly rewarding and relaxing when you reach your destination.

And the intended relationship with the customer:

> I want to build something that will have die hard fans who trust every thing i put out.

Use these as the internal standard for respecting the customer's investment. The public page expresses them through comfort at the destination, patient product development, and trust earned through repeated use.

## Source handling

Images on the Vision page are unchanged copies of existing JPEG posters in `../jn-wilderness-selection/previews/`. No source video, original image, Final Cut library, or synced project file was modified. Exact paths and clip ranges are recorded in [vision-media-sources.json](vision-media-sources.json).

The selected interval locates the source clip. The original poster's exact frame timestamp was not recorded, so it must not be described as a precisely timed frame. Inventory directory dates are not asserted as trip dates. Locations follow the archive's existing event and selection mapping.

The original archive catalog is `../jn-wilderness-selection/selections.json`; the browsable gallery is `../jn-wilderness-selection/index.html`. Paths here are relative to the storefront root. The gallery's missing-media and sampling limitations remain in its `READ-ME.txt`.
