# Capra core vision

Updated October 20, 2026. This is the current reference for product direction, brand voice, and the website. It supersedes the generic outdoor athleisure framing in the original storefront scaffold.

## The feeling at the center

Capra begins in the Washington backcountry: effort shared with friends, changing weather, rough terrain, and the pleasure of arriving somewhere you want to stay. The defining moment is reaching the destination and feeling deeply glad you invested your hard-earned money in gear that helps you enjoy being there.

The ambition is to feel at home in wild places. Capable layers should let the wearer move naturally, stay comfortable within the garment's intended conditions, and settle into the landscape with the people they care about. The product has to respect the money, time, and trust someone puts into it.

Health and individual strength are part of this vision. Effort is approached with heart, curiosity, and the joy of discovering what your body can do. Companionship, warmth, and relaxation belong alongside demanding movement and terrain.

## Who it is for

People with an orientation toward nature who want a full, active life outdoors. The shared quality is a desire to participate, explore, and return. Experience can grow over time. The brand should speak with the specificity of lived outdoor experience while remaining welcoming to people finding their way into it.

The founder's ambition is a devoted community whose trust strengthens with repeated use. Success means people reach for their Capra clothes again, recommend them from experience, and have good reason to consider the next release.

## What the clothes must work toward

- **Freedom of movement:** natural high steps, crouching, reaching, scrambling, and deep knee flexion. A comfortable relationship between the body, cut, seams, and layers.
- **Durability through real use:** construction and materials chosen with repeated rock contact, scree, pack friction, and snow travel in mind. Glissading and sliding are founder use cases to investigate, with garment-specific limits and suitable testing.
- **Comfort through changing effort:** consider exertion, moisture, weather, and the transition to rest at the destination together.
- **Material integrity:** select and substantiate fabrics and construction for their intended purpose. Minimal presentation must carry the same care as the object itself.
- **Considered form:** fashion informs proportions, silhouette, color, texture, and restraint. Every fabric, color, fit, and form should have an explainable reason.
- **Responsibility over time:** develop patiently, learn from wear and failure, and define support and repair commitments that the business can deliver.

These are development standards. No garment's weather protection, lifespan, safety, or abrasion performance has been established by this vision. Product claims require product-specific evidence. The current catalog remains a clearly labeled prototype with sample specifications.

## The visual world

Use real landscapes, real outdoor moments, and the people within them. Washington is the origin of the vision. Trips elsewhere can extend the story with their actual locations identified. The current homepage uses a chronological montage of attributed archival and Creative Commons footage. It celebrates historical achievement; it does not document the founder's trips or Capra garment testing.

The phrase “ready for foreign planets” describes the feeling of capability and possibility. The website direction is documentary: actual terrain, weather, friends, and garments. Photography should retain the character of those places.

The supplied mountain goat and CAPRA sketch remains the identity. The rebuilt homepage on `capra-jc-1` uses a dark, minimal composition inspired by Known.com, a centered statement, and a film of real historical feats cut every half second. This composition was developed independently of the earlier storefront. Continue improving it on `capra-jc-1`; earlier iterations remain in Git history. ROA helped the founder identify a standard of coherence, fashion sensitivity, material care, and product visibility. Capra's purpose comes from the founder's own outdoor experience.

## The website concept

The long-term shopping concept begins with environments. A visitor selects a landscape and enters a scene where a crew wears the collection. Discreet, accessible garment markers reveal product details while preserving the sense of place.

The garment must remain easy to see and understand. Close views, fit in motion, material information, intended conditions, and the reason for each choice should substantiate the feeling. Direct collection browsing remains available. The current homepage uses an expanding film frame to convey exhilaration; provide a reduced-motion equivalent and a still image when video is unavailable.

The current implementation has an **Our story** link to `/vision`, alongside Clothing, Accessories, Studio and Bag in a visible top navigation. All pages share the homepage’s dark palette, warm white typography, transparent sketch and restrained controls. The homepage’s **Explore** button leads to the collection. Environment selection and shoppable people remain future work; the homepage film does not demonstrate garments or their performance.

## The goat and the studio

**Free-spirited by nature.** The goat is a symbol of curiosity and independent exploration: following your own instinct, wandering beyond the familiar path, and finding your own way. Climbing suggests resolve: meeting difficult ground with patience, precision and the drive to reach higher. Life on exposed ground suggests resourcefulness: adapting and making the most of what is there. The Studio page connects these three ideas—independence, resolve and resourcefulness—to the original hand-drawn mark. They express the brand’s outlook rather than verified garment capabilities.

## Voice

Write with warmth, specificity, and conviction. Refer to the pack coming off, snow at a creek crossing, a knee bending over rock, or friends making camp. Keep the joy of being there present alongside the demands of getting there.

Use founder quotations accurately and attribute them as founder statements. Describe the ambition as an ambition until the products establish the evidence. Explain quality through decisions, construction, testing, and use.
